Cole Calfee

I lead brand, marketing, and business development at Freeman General Contracting, an Atlanta-based builder on its way to becoming one of the best hospitality builders in America.

My work is shaping how Freeman is seen, how we tell our story, and who we build alongside.

I'm drawn to the places where business, art, and the built environment meet, and to the people who take all three seriously. Before Freeman, I was a creative director in Nashville's music industry, after studying at the University of Georgia.

I also run Tangerine, an independent studio working with a small number of artists and brands.

I want to make things that are unmistakably good, with people who care deeply about their work, and build what lasts.
