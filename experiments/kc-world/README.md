# KC'S WORLD — EXPERIMENT 001

Private-in-spirit, unpublished concept for Kashus Culpepper's future website, featuring the **original Little KC** character.

## Status
- Visual master: the OG Little KC artwork supplied by Cole on October 16, 2026: textured red afro, surprised face, red cowboy linework, blue trousers, hat held in left hand, red devil balloon and blue halo balloon.
- The *original uploaded image* is the sole authoritative source, not earlier redraws or AI-generated substitutes.
- Source image is retained in the local working bundle until binary asset upload; **not yet committed to this Git branch**. Do not claim otherwise.
- No production website modifications, no live deployment.
- Team briefs: [CLYDE.md](./CLYDE.md), [JIM.md](./JIM.md), [TOM.md](./TOM.md).

## Creative north star
Less website. More world. Sophisticated warm-paper editorial site, KC as a hand-drawn host rather than an irritating mascot. Visitors get to music, tour dates and merchandise immediately. Character moments are brief, playful, optional, accessible and never interrupt conversion.

## Motion sequence (first milestone)
0.0–1.0s: cream paper frame, sound muted by default, key navigation visible.
1.0–2.8s: red and blue balloons enter gently from top. KC floats after them.
2.8–4.1s: KC slows; his legs trail, hands hold balloon strings and hat.
4.1–5.2s: one eye movement and curious look toward the visitor.
5.2–6.5s: wordmark reveals; KC settles.
6.5–8.0s: idle loop with subtle balloon sway. User can immediately click MUSIC / SHOWS / GOODS / STORY.

## Non-negotiables
1. Preserve original proportions, textures, red afro, facial expression, costume, hat and balloon symbolism.
2. Never repaint into Pixar/3D/modern vector animation; do not replace facial features.
3. Use lossless transparent cutouts from the original source; explicit visual QC at 200% scale.
4. Make short deterministic animation, not unpredictable generative runtime animation.
5. All links and controls functional; no fabricated tour dates, merch, music or ticket URLs.
6. Honor reduced-motion; no forced audio, splash screens, long waits, scrolljacking, or blocking navigation.
7. Desktop and mobile with no horizontal overflow, lightweight assets, responsive sizes.
8. Keep isolated from live Kash and portfolio site until Cole explicitly approves.

## Review criteria
First prove original character likeness and the feeling of gentle buoyancy. Only then build interacting sections.
