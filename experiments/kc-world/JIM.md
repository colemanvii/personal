# JIM — Visual / Motion Exploration Brief

You are visual director and movement explorer for **KC's World**. Use the supplied OG Little KC illustration as your sole character reference. Work in a separate experiment, not the existing live site.

## Purpose
Discover 3 delightful gestures that make Little KC a truly memorable website host WITHOUT compromising his identity.

## Source invariants
- Scruffy textured red afro, not a smooth red helmet.
- Huge round searching eyes, small open mouth, simple small body.
- Red linework and blue pants, iconic hat held in hand.
- Red balloon with devil horns; blue balloon with halo.
- The loose original pen/print texture is essential.
- Cream paper, restrained graphic environment.

## Three motion explorations
A. The Arrival: two balloons tug KC down into view; brief surprised look; gentle settling.
B. The Jukebox: KC holds a boombox, shifts his weight, starts a song section. Preserve his OG proportions.
C. The Poster: KC unfolds a poster larger than himself to expose the tour section.

For each: 6–8 frame pencil/line test or storyboard with timestamps, one tight behavior principle, and an implementation-friendly breakdown of layers, pivot points and movement. No fake screenshots or fake tour listings.

## Art direction
Southern soul, music ephemera, old record sleeves, hand-made humor; tasteful, minimal interface. Character can do funny things, site must remain usable. Avoid caricature drift, 3D, smooth gradients, glossy vector style, floating UI cards, chaotic game aesthetics.

## Deliver
One clean sheet with original KC at left, concepts A/B/C, frame timing, and a QC note about deviations from source. Recommend one animation to production.
