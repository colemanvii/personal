Plan,

Elevation

Three different ideas about an artist's website. Plan is how a stranger meets the work. Section is where the work accumulates and is kept honest. Elevation is a private wall where it gets made. Each one is built and works. All three use the same 72 records and Cole's existing images.

Public experiencePlan

A way in

A showing is a curated entrance: a title, a checklist, then one work at a time with a proper wall label. If you stay on a work for a few seconds, the label opens onto the rest of the work. Sit lets the showing turn by itself. All 72 records are one click away. There are no 3D rooms and no closing dates.

Creative workspaceElevation

A finite wall

A private wall, 12 ft × 7 ft, drawn as an elevation. Paintings hang at their recorded sizes, and everything else at a marked working size. The wall can't grow: to put something up, take something down. Everything else waits in the library. Snapshots record the wall with the date, and one can be proposed for the public site. Nothing is published from here.

  • Originals are never touched. Every image here is a resized copy. The painting crops are made from the photographs, which stay unchanged, and every crop is listed in canvases.json.

How facts are marked

Known, inferred, missing

Counts are from the 72 records. Sources are the live site's own files.

Verified

Stated for this work in a named source, such as a painting's size in paintings-archive.js.

Inferred

Taken from a source but not stated for this work. Most dates are the project's year from the Index.

Study label

A working name given here because none is recorded, such as "Ronnie Hodges 01". Plan shows these as Untitled.

Reading

An interpretation by Claude, such as shared motifs. Unconfirmed.

Missing

Not recorded anywhere available: 27 dates, 58 sizes, every collector, every original link.

Notes

What's real and what isn't

The showings Red, Ranch & Arena and Paper are selections made for this study. Their titles are working titles. Their wall text is built from facts alone (counts, kinds, years) and is marked as a placeholder for Cole's own.

No provenance is invented. Sold paintings say Sold and nothing more. "Where now" is recorded as missing on every painting, and collector information is never inferred.

Authorship gaps are shown. Seven images on the site have no stated photographer, including the Kashus portraits, PBR and the studio photograph. Their records say so.

Elevation is private by design. It saves only to the browser it's used in. Snapshots and proposals never leave it. In the full system, a proposal becomes a draft showing for review.

Music, film and writing have lanes in Section, although the repository holds only one music credit, one film credit and no writing. The lanes are ready, and they don't pretend to be full.

Built for Generations appears as Freeman records in Section, with the client named on each one. It isn't on the Elevation wall, and it isn't a showing unless Cole makes it one.
