# Editorial standard

Every candidate drawing is reviewed against this before it can enter the 80. Draw more
than you need: plan on **~120 finished drawings for 80 places** (the 1/40 especially —
two or three sittings per station are normal). Cut work is archived, never destroyed,
and never sold as part of the set.

## Hard gates (any fail = redraw, never repair in software)

1. **Authorship.** Drawn by Cole's hand on paper. No AI-generated or AI-traced imagery,
   no projected tracing of a study. The geometry template may be used under the sheet;
   that is the only allowed aid. Process photo exists (see `../provenance/`).
2. **Geometry.** Passes `tools/geometry_check.py` by eye at full size: left eye keeps
   its square corner; eyes touch each other and the mouth; mouth top flat; one-unit neck;
   torso on the bottom edge; four units wide, centred. Deliberate departures are allowed
   only if written into the registry *before* drawing (e.g. Sta. 38, drawn blind).
3. **Avatar legibility.** At 64 px the figure is recognisable as Opepen without being
   told. There is a value contrast between figure and ground, not only a contour.
4. **No costume.** Nothing is worn by the figure. No hats, bandanas, boots, badges.
5. **Archival.** Lightfast pigment (ASTM I/II), acid-free cotton paper, no tape on the
   image, no correction fluid.

## Scored criteria (1–5 each; enter the set at 20+ with nothing below 3)

| Criterion | The question | A 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Idea** | Does the silhouette do work here, or is it a cookie-cutter? | Figure and subject need each other; remove either and the drawing collapses. |
| **Originality** | Is it a pun, a cliché, or a repeat of another piece in the set or another Opepen set? | You can't name what it's "like". |
| **Expressiveness** | Is the hand alive? Are there decisions you can see? | Varied line speed and pressure; a mark you'd point at. |
| **Craft** | Is it drawn well — animal anatomy, perspective, hatching control, ink handling? | Would stand next to the sporting/natural-history masters it cites. |
| **Standalone value** | Cover the word Opepen. Would you frame it? | A stranger would buy it at a drawing show. |
| **Contribution** | What does it give the set that nothing else does? (registry `unique` column) | Removing it leaves a hole. |

## Group-level checks (before any group is considered complete)

- The group's purpose is visible in the contact sheet without captions.
- No two pieces share the same relationship to the silhouette *and* the same density.
- Range: each group of 10+ includes spare, mid and dense work.
- The 1/40 gets the same review time per piece as the 1/1 gets per hour.
- Family resemblance with the other five groups survives at contact-sheet size.

## Set-level checks (before submission)

- Read the 80 as a sequence: does the walk from altitude to ground make sense?
- Compare against the completed Opepen set survey (`../research/03-set-survey.md`).
- The 1/1 earns its rarity by hours and ambition, not by a gimmick.
- Any doubt about a piece → it is cut. The set is better at 80 strong than 80 complete.
